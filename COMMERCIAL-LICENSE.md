# Commercial licensing

The public source license does not grant commercial-use rights. Organizations that want to embed Tomo Streaming, offer it as a service, resell it, or use it for commercial advantage must obtain a written license from Tomo.

The commercial agreement can cover self-hosted deployments, managed Tomo Streaming Cloud capacity, support, SLAs and redistribution rights.

To request commercial terms, open an issue or contact the maintainers through the [Tomo Social organization](https://github.com/Tomo-Social). Include the repositories, deployment model, expected scale and whether you need hosted capacity or redistribution rights.
